What are Agent Skills?

Agent skills are modular capabilities that perform focused tasks—think of them as plugins that let agents act on behalf of users within well-defined boundaries.

Design Constraints

Skills should be safe, auditable, and have clear input/output contracts. Keep network and permission boundaries explicit.

Interaction Patterns

Pick the right interaction: quizzes for recall, scenarios for decision-making, timelines for sequence, and drag-and-drop for categorization.

Measuring Mastery

Use objective-mapped questions and confidence ratings to estimate mastery per objective. Persist results for review and certificate generation.
